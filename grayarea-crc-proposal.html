A fully operational AI-powered intelligence platform built specifically for Capital Region Co's sales and marketing teams — aggregating NY OCM license data, Pistol market data, CRM activity, and internal sales data into a single live command center.

01 — Scope of Work

What We're Building

Six core systems, built from scratch and integrated into a single platform. Everything your sales and marketing teams need to move faster, waste less time, and never miss a new license again.

01
NY OCM License Intelligence
Automated monitoring of the NY Office of Cannabis Management portal. New licenses, application stage advances, ownership changes, and revocations are captured in real time and pushed to your dashboard and alert system — no more manual checks.
Web Scraper Auto Alerts NY OCM Live Feed
02
Pistol Data Integration
Direct connection to your Pistol account via API. Market share data, retailer performance, and competitive assortment data flows automatically into the dashboard on a scheduled sync — giving your team live market context alongside every account.
API Integration Market Data Scheduled Sync
03
CRM Sync
Two-way integration with your CRM (HubSpot or Salesforce). Deal stages, contact history, rep assignments, and activity logs sync bidirectionally — so reps can work from the dashboard without double-entering data into two systems.
HubSpot / Salesforce Two-Way Sync Activity Logs
04
AI Prospect Scoring
Every account receives a live AI score (0–100) calculated from license recency, contact history, order frequency, geography, and market signals. The AI surfaces your highest-value opportunities daily and flags at-risk accounts before they churn.
Anthropic API Scoring Model Churn Detection
05
AI Chat Assistant
A natural language interface trained on your actual data. Ask it anything — "which accounts should I call today," "what's happening in the Albany market," "draft an outreach email for Upstate Green" — and get answers grounded in real CRC data, not generic AI responses.
Anthropic Claude RAG Architecture Live Context
06
Dashboard & Alert System
The full production version of the command center mockup — all five views fully functional with live data. Plus a configurable alert engine: set rules for any trigger (new license, stage advance, days since contact) and receive notifications via dashboard, email, or Slack.
5 Dashboard Views Alert Engine Email / Slack Role-Based Access
